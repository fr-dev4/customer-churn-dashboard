KPI definitions

Churn rate = churned customers / customers in view.

Retention rate = 1 − churn rate.

Revenue at risk = MonthlyCharges of churned customers (lost MRR); annualised = ×12. Also shown as % of total MRR in view.

Tenure KPIs = average TenureMonths overall, and for churned vs retained customers.

Tenure bands: 0–6, 7–12, 13–24, 25+ months.

Summary of findings

All 7 month-to-month customers churned; all 8 customers on one- or two-year contracts stayed. Churn is concentrated in the first 14 months of tenure and in customers raising 3+ support tickets. Lost MRR is $409.93 of $1,269.85 (32%). Recommended actions: offer month-to-month customers an annual-plan incentive, add a proactive check-in during months 1–12, and escalate customers after their third ticket. Caveat: with 15 rows, contract, tenure and tickets overlap heavily, so their effects cannot be separated.
